How I Work

My Process

From idea
to launch,
with care.

Every project is a collaboration. I believe in transparent communication, iterative design, and engineering that scales.

01
Discovery & Strategy
Deep-dive into your goals, audience, and competitors. We define scope, set KPIs, and architect the right solution before a single line of code is written.
02
Design & Prototyping
High-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma. We validate UX flows, motion concepts, and brand expression before development begins.
03
Development & Animation
Pixel-perfect implementation with GSAP, React, and modern tooling. Attention to performance, accessibility (WCAG AA), and browser compatibility throughout.
04
Launch & Beyond
Rigorous QA, SEO optimization, and a smooth handoff. Post-launch support to monitor performance and iterate based on real user data.
